Glossary

Key terms needed to understand the data and the story.

Residential Care

Also called a nursing home or aged care facility. An elderly person moves in permanently (or for short-term respite). Staff provide 24/7 care on-site. In this project we count beds (residential places) and users at the SA3 level. This is the "supply" side of the story.

Home Care (HCP)

Home Care Package — government-funded support delivered to a person living at home (cleaning, nursing visits, meals, transport). The person stays in their own house. There are 4 levels based on care complexity. This is the "demand" side — especially Level 3–4 users who likely need residential care but can't access it.

HCP Levels 1–4

Home Care Package levels
L1 Basic care needs — ~$10,000/year subsidy. Minimal support (cleaning, social visits).
L2 Low care needs — ~$18,000/year. Regular nursing, personal care.
L3 Intermediate needs — ~$40,000/year. Frequent complex care. Proxy for residential waitlist.
L4 High care needs — ~$56,000/year. Near nursing-home level care. Should be in residential but waiting.
L3 + L4 together = hcp_high_needs — the hidden demand indicator in this project.

Star Ratings

ACQSC rates every residential facility 1–5 stars across 4 dimensions. Published quarterly since 2023.

★ 1 starSignificant concerns — regulatory action
★★ 2 starsBelow acceptable standard
★★★ 3 starsAcceptable — meeting basic requirements
★★★★ 4 starsGood
★★★★★ 5 starsExcellent
4 sub-dimensions: Residents' Experience, Compliance, Staffing, Quality Measures. We average these into quality_score. National average Feb 2026: 3.65.

SA3 — Statistical Area Level 3

Australia's standard geographic unit for regional analysis. ~358 SA3 regions nationally, ranging from inner-city suburbs to vast remote areas. Each SA3 has a unique 5-digit code (e.g. 10102 = Queanbeyan). This project uses SA3 as the main join key — all maps, charts, and comparisons are at SA3 level. Smaller than a state, bigger than a suburb.

ACPR — Aged Care Planning Region

73 larger regions used by the Department of Health for aged care planning. Cannot be joined to SA3 — they are different geographic systems. ACPR data (Indigenous, NESB demographics from CURF) must be analysed separately. Never mix SA3 and ACPR in the same chart.

MMM — Modified Monash Model

Remoteness classification used by the Australian government for health workforce policy.

MM1Major city
MM2Regional centre (large)
MM3Large rural town
MM4Medium rural town
MM5Small rural town — most overrepresented in declining quality
MM6Remote community
MM7Very remote community

Care Minutes Mandate

From 1 Oct 2023, every residential facility must deliver a minimum amount of direct care per resident per day:

Oct 2023 target200 min total / 40 min registered nurse
Oct 2024 target (raised)215 min total / 44 min RN
Targets are facility-specific (ACQSC adjusts per resident acuity). fully_compliant = True means actual ≥ target for both RN and total minutes.

Organisation Types

Who operates the facility:

For-profit — private companies, publicly listed corporations. Motivated by shareholder returns. Avg quality 3.68.
Not-for-profit — charities, religious organisations, community groups. Surplus reinvested into care. Avg quality 3.80.
Government — state/local government operated. Avg quality 4.21.

Data Rules

Hard Rules — never violate

🚫
Never edit data/clean/ files manually. Regenerate by running the corresponding pipeline notebook.
🚫
Never mix SA3 and ACPR grains in the same metric or chart. SA3 ≠ ACPR.
🚫
Exclude SA3 10702 (Illawarra Catchment Reserve) from all ratio metrics — pop_65_plus = 0 (conservation land, not a data error).
🚫
quality_score = mean of exactly 4 sub-ratings: residents_exp, staffing, compliance, quality_measures. Do not include overall_rating.
🚫
Do not present remote areas as lower quality. MM5 = 4.05 vs MM1 = 3.75. The remote disadvantage is access, not quality.

Soft Rules — best practice

💡
All written output must be in English — notebooks, markdown files, commit messages. Vietnamese is for conversation only.
💡
Every claim must have a number. "Quality improved" is not a finding. "Quality rose +0.25 pts (3.40 → 3.65) after Oct 2023" is a finding.
💡
Join on sa3_code (canonical key). Drop rows where sa3_code is null or non-numeric before joining.
💡
Negative funding values are valid (clawback/reconciliation). Do not zero them out.

Common Gotchas

2023 files: lowercase; 2024+ files: Title Case — ORG_MAP misses half the data
IssueWhat happensFix
NaN >= target Evaluates to False in pandas — pre-mandate rows wrongly counted as non-compliant Use .where(has_actual) to preserve NaN
SA3 10702 zero pop Divide-by-zero → inf in ratio metrics Filter sa3_code != 10702 before any ratio calculation
MMM ordering MM1, MM10, MM2… alphabetic sort breaks charts str.extract(r'(\d)') to get numeric digit for ordering
Purpose column capitalisation .str.strip().str.lower() before mapping
add_vline on datetime axis TypeError with date string Use pd.Timestamp('2023-10-01').timestamp() * 1000
ACQSC revised ratings Same facility appears 2× in one snapshot (mid-quarter revision) Dedup key: snapshot + Service Name + state + acpr_name, keep='first'
