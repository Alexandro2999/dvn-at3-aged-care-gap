Australia's Aged-Care Gap — Pitch Delivery v3

Slide-first, narrator-light · QR-invite at S9 · 11 slides · ~4:30 total

🎯 Target ~4:30 (breathing room for Q&A) 📊 Data refreshed 2026-05-13 🎙 Voice complements visual — never duplicates

4 delivery principles

  1. Numbers on the slide → don't read them. Audiences read faster than you can speak. Voice carries story, not narration. The only exception: S7 human-scale numbers (56 / 18) are read slowly, intentionally, to convey weight.
  2. Voice = story + emphasis. Lines like "Here's the paradox", "This isn't a queue, it's a trap", "Execution matters" live only in the spoken voice — never on the slide.
  3. Pause is a weapon. A 2-second silence after a hook lets the audience study the slide. S3 and S7 have built-in pauses.
  4. S9 = brief mention + QR invite, NOT a live demo. The audience explores the dashboard themselves after the pitch. Reason: saves ~28s and removes tech risk. A backup demo flow is ready if the Q&A asks.

Q&A Backpocket

6 likely questions · keep each answer ≤ 30 seconds

Q1: "Why is MM7 (very remote) higher quality than MM1 (cities)?"
"Two structural reasons. One — for-profit operators avoid MM6/7; remote facilities are almost entirely government or NFP, which score higher across the board. Two — remote facilities are smaller, embedded in tight communities, face less pressure to cut staff costs. The pattern holds within every year of data — not a sample artifact."
Q2: "Is the for-profit gap just confounded by location or case mix?"
"We tested that. The 0.57-star gap holds inside every remoteness band — MM1 cities through MM5 rural towns. In MM5 it actually widens. If it were location-confounded, the gap would shrink in MM1 where for-profits cluster. It doesn't."
Q3: "How did you forecast 2025?"
"Only population is projected — three scenarios: current ABS trend, faster aging at +4%/year, or no growth. Quality, supply, home care all use real 2025 data from latest GEN release. We didn't model quality or supply because they depend on policy enforcement — unpredictable. Population has 5 years of baseline — we can project honestly."
Q4: "What's a 'Care Gap Index'? Did you invent it?"
"Yes. Our metric: residential access rate ÷ quality score. Higher = more demand pressure per quality star. Captures both sides simultaneously — a region high on demand but low on quality scores high; a region with low demand but high quality scores low. Single-axis metrics hide regions that fail on both."
Q5: "Where does the '82 crisis zones' number come from?"
"Waitlist Pressure > 1.0 — more high-needs home-care users than residential beds available, per SA3. Latest 2025 data. 82 SA3s cross the threshold, up from 58 a year earlier. List is regenerable in the dashboard — filter to your state."
Q6: "What if our region isn't on the top-10?"
"Filter to your state — the top-10 movers list is state-aware. Pick Tasmania, you get worst Tasmanian regions; pick NSW, worst NSW. The 82 zones span every state except ACT — no state where this isn't a problem."
