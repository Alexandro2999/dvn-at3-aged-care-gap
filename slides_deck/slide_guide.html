Q&A Preparation — 1.5 minutes

Anticipate these 4 questions from the rubric
⏱ 5:00 – 6:30

"Isn't remote always worse?"

No — MM7 scores 3.90 stars, the highest quality of any band. The remote problem is access (distance, scarcity), not quality.

"Are you saying for-profit is bad?"

We're saying ownership is associated with lower quality inside every geography band tested. A 0.53-star gap stable for 3 years warrants the question.

"Has the mandate worked?"

Staffing improved (+0.51 pts). Resident health outcomes moved −0.015 pts. The jury is still out on whether improved inputs translate to better lives.

"Why aren't beds going to crisis zones?"

The data doesn't tell us why — only that it's happening. The 58 crisis zones lost 551 beds while the rest added 12,027. That is a market failure in geographic allocation.
